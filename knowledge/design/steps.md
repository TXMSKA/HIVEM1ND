# Protocol steps

What each protocol step checks, in a few words, so a pass can pick its steps from the [routing table](INDEX.md#routing) without opening every protocol. A protocol file is opened only when at least one of its steps will actually run; a step whose subject the surface does not have ends as not applicable from its line here. Each protocol file holds the task, the time and the result that counts as evidence.

- [accessibility](protocols/accessibility.md): 1 native elements for buttons, links and grids; 2 keyboard walk, forward and back; 3 visible focus never covered; 4 target sizes; 5 contrast pairs; 6 text spacing, reflow at 320 and zoom; 7 skip link, landmarks, heading order and title.
- [assets-and-media](protocols/assets-and-media.md): 1 functional icons from one published set; 2 inline themeable SVG with accessible names; 3 icons only where they replace reading; 4 image format, size and weight; 5 largest paint priority and lazy loading; 6 self-hosted fonts actually loaded; 7 invented placeholders and orphaned files.
- [colour-and-theming](protocols/colour-and-theming.md): 1 hierarchy without hue; 2 colour on semantic tokens, literals searched; 3 contrast table in the light theme; 4 second theme re-pointed and measured; 5 hover, active, focus and selected readable; 6 a second cue beside colour; 7 shadows, borders and elevation.
- [composition-and-layout](protocols/composition-and-layout.md): 1 what the first viewport carries; 2 material mapped to geometry; 3 objects on the shared grid; 4 gaps on three bands; 5 a job for every container; 6 repeated sections; 7 overflow at every breakpoint.
- [design-specificity](protocols/design-specificity.md): 1 subject, audience and job named; 2 the obvious version written down; 3 defaults rewritten, or sorted as in [defaults.md](defaults.md), In a refine; 4 template chrome searches; 5 palette and type against the clusters; 6 dominant object with text masked; 7 boldness spent once.
- [forms-and-inputs](protocols/forms-and-inputs.md): 1 each control against the control table; 2 visible labels, no helper sentences; 3 presses on dates and long lists; 4 menus open, anchored and visible; 5 a reason on every disabled control; 6 errors in place, focus and values kept; 7 submit progress, double press and success; 8 input size, type, mode and autocomplete on a phone.
- [hierarchy-and-type](protocols/hierarchy-and-type.md): 1 every text on a named role; 2 free sizes outside the roles; 3 characters per line; 4 largest size, tracking floor, and the display role on a Persuade section; 5 tabular numerals on compared figures; 6 vertical rhythm on three bands; 7 outline and stranded words.
- [interface-states](protocols/interface-states.md): 1 inventory of reachable states; 2 a capture of each; 3 a way forward on every empty and error state; 4 feedback, skeletons and success; 5 live regions for what appears on its own; 6 undo or confirmation on destructive paths; 7 empty, long and large content.
- [motion](protocols/motion.md): 1 every animation through the gates; 2 durations and curves; 3 transform, opacity and clip path only; 4 retrigger and interruption; 5 text not held back by reveals; 6 reduced motion honoured; 7 slow-motion check and a pause on long loops.
- [responsive-behaviour](protocols/responsive-behaviour.md): 1 the widths to test; 2 overflow at each width; 3 arrangement changes before scale; 4 shortest, longest and extreme real content; 5 touch targets and a 16 px input font; 6 alternatives to gestures; 7 safe areas and sticky regions.
- [theme-direction](protocols/theme-direction.md): 1 the direction as one sentence; 2 what the direction may move; 3 new primitives and a real display face; 4 components unchanged in the diff; 5 contrast recomputed per theme; 6 keyboard walk repeated; 7 the direction spent on one element.
- [visual-critique](protocols/visual-critique.md): 1 captures at three widths and each key state; 2 written judgment before numbers; 3 seams where new work meets old; 4 the primary task walked as personas; 5 findings ranked and P0 and P1 fixed; 6 a fresh-eyes verdict.

A refine of a whole surface starts from the floor steps: accessibility 1 to 7, colour-and-theming 2, 3, 5 and 6, hierarchy-and-type 1 to 3, interface-states 1 to 4 and 6, responsive-behaviour 2 and 5, motion 3 and 6, assets-and-media 1 and 2, and visual-critique 1, 2, 5 and 6. Any other step runs only when the map or a search from [defaults.md](defaults.md), Searches, hits its subject.
