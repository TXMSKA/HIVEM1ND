# Long form

Text read from start to end: articles, posts and announcements. This is the category where machine-written habits are most visible, because there is room for all of them. Defaults for the whole category: Plain tone, prose line shape, dash policy none (cap when a house style keeps the dash), sentence length variance above the threshold in [measures.md](../measures.md). Every subcategory runs [tell-removal](../protocols/tell-removal.md) and [copy-verification](../protocols/copy-verification.md).

## Articles

Applies when: a blog post, an essay, a case study, a guide written as an article, "make this read less like AI", a draft over three hundred words.

Options: impersonal or first person plural when a team signs it. Prose over lists. A last updated date where the age of the content matters.

Open: [pages.md, Articles](../pages.md#articles), [tells.md](../tells.md), and [claim-check](../protocols/claim-check.md) when the article asserts results.

## Announcements

Applies when: a launch post, a news item, a policy or pricing change notice, a service status update, a message to all users.

Options: result first, first person plural when a team signs it, short block for the essentials and prose only after them. Any change to terms links the terms rather than restating them.

Open: [pages.md, Announcements](../pages.md#announcements), [boundaries.md](../boundaries.md), and [claim-check](../protocols/claim-check.md).
