# Marketing and product pages

Pages that have to say what a thing is and why it matters to someone deciding. Defaults for the whole category: Plain tone (Promotional only when a campaign asks for it by name), claim level verified only, dash policy none, no emoji (see [options.md](../options.md)). Its protocols are in the [routing table](../INDEX.md#routing).

## Hero

Applies when: a landing page, a homepage headline or subheadline, "rewrite the hero", the first screen of a product page, a positioning change.

Build: headline naming the outcome in plain nouns; one sentence *{Product} is {what} for {who}*, with the name or the missing-fact marker; one primary button equal to the heading of its destination.

Options: second person or impersonal; one headline and one sentence beneath it.

Open: [pages.md, Hero](../pages.md#hero).

## Feature sections

Applies when: a features grid, a benefits list, a section per capability, "describe what it does".

Build: per block, a heading that starts with a verb and states the outcome: *See who has paid*; one or two sentences on how, only for features that exist today.

Options: second person or impersonal; one or two sentences per feature.

Open: [pages.md, Feature sections](../pages.md#feature-sections).

## Pricing

Applies when: a pricing table, plan names, a comparison of tiers, a price change, a billing page, a button that charges or upgrades a plan.

Build: a table with one row per plan: name, who it is for, price with currency and period, what it includes and excludes. Every unknown value is the missing-fact marker, never a plausible number. Taxes, fees, shipping, the trial's end and the renewal price are stated beside the button that charges.

Options: impersonal, table line shape for the comparison; contractual terms are filler only (see [boundaries.md](../boundaries.md#filler-only)).

Open: [pages.md, Pricing](../pages.md#pricing).

## Calls to action

Applies when: a sign-up, trial, contact or buy button on a page, a banner asking for an action, "make the CTA stronger".

Build: verb and object equal to the destination heading: *Create your first invoice* leads to a page titled *Create your first invoice*. One primary per view. A button with no destination keeps its label and is listed as an open item.

Options: no person, fragment length.

Open: [pages.md, Calls to action](../pages.md#calls-to-action) and [microcopy.md, Buttons and controls](../microcopy.md#buttons-and-controls).

## Proof and claims

Applies when: testimonials, customer logos, figures, awards, comparisons with competitors, any superlative, a screenshot slot without a screenshot.

Build: only proof that exists, with its source recorded; a slot without it holds the missing-fact marker as visible text. Ratings, review counts, badges and compliance claims trace to a source or a document; reviews the owner wrote or paid for are labelled or removed. A quote already on the page stays verbatim and is listed for the owner when no record of it is at hand.

Options: claim level verified only.

Open: [pages.md, Proof and claims](../pages.md#proof-and-claims) and [claim-check](../protocols/claim-check.md).

## Footers

Applies when: footer text, a line about cookies, privacy or tracking outside the policy page, a copyright line.

Build: links in sentence case with the words of the heading they open (*Privacy policy*, *Terms of service*); a destination heading in title case is the owner's text, so it is listed for the owner rather than copied into the link. Then the copyright line with the year and the owner's name or markers, and no sentence restating a policy. The footer links every notice the product needs (privacy policy, terms, refund policy, cookie notice with a way to reopen the choice) and carries the business details or links them; the text of each is the owner's.

Options: impersonal, fragment or one sentence, linking to the policy rather than paraphrasing it.

Open: [pages.md, Footers](../pages.md#footers) and [boundaries.md, Consistency instead of rewrite](../boundaries.md#consistency-instead-of-rewrite).
