# Marketing and product pages

Pages that have to say what a thing is and why it matters to someone deciding. Defaults for the whole category: Plain tone (Promotional only when a campaign asks for it by name), claim level verified only, dash policy none, no emoji (see [options.md](../options.md)). Every subcategory runs [surface-copy](../protocols/surface-copy.md), [tell-removal](../protocols/tell-removal.md) and [claim-check](../protocols/claim-check.md).

## Hero

Applies when: a landing page, a homepage headline or subheadline, "rewrite the hero", the first screen of a product page, a positioning change.

Options: second person or impersonal, one headline and one sentence beneath it. The first sentence names what and who.

Open: [pages.md, Hero](../pages.md#hero).

## Feature sections

Applies when: a features grid, a benefits list, a section per capability, "describe what it does".

Options: verb-first outcome headings, one or two sentences per feature, one feature per block.

Open: [pages.md, Feature sections](../pages.md#feature-sections).

## Pricing

Applies when: a pricing table, plan names, a comparison of tiers, a price change, a billing page.

Options: impersonal, table line shape for the comparison, every price with period and currency. Terms that are contractual are filler only (see [boundaries.md](../boundaries.md)).

Open: [pages.md, Pricing](../pages.md#pricing).

## Calls to action

Applies when: a sign-up, trial, contact or buy button on a page, a banner asking for an action, "make the CTA stronger".

Options: verb and object, the label matching the destination heading, one primary per view.

Open: [pages.md, Calls to action](../pages.md#calls-to-action) and [microcopy.md, Buttons and controls](../microcopy.md#buttons-and-controls).

## Proof and claims

Applies when: testimonials, customer logos, figures, awards, comparisons with competitors, any superlative, a screenshot slot without a screenshot.

Options: claim level verified only. A missing proof stays a visible gap.

Open: [pages.md, Proof and claims](../pages.md#proof-and-claims) and [claim-check](../protocols/claim-check.md).

## Footers

Applies when: footer text, a line about cookies, privacy or tracking outside the policy page, a copyright line.

Options: impersonal, fragment or one sentence, linking to the policy rather than paraphrasing it.

Open: [pages.md, Footers](../pages.md#footers) and [boundaries.md](../boundaries.md).
