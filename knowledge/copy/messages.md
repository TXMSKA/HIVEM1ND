# Messages

Rules for text addressed to a person rather than shown on a page: email, notifications, and status reports or replies. Each section is one subcategory. Every rule here is Convention unless marked.

## Email

- The subject says what the email is about or what it asks: *Invoice 2041 is overdue*, not *Update*.
- The first line of the body is the point. No opening about hoping the message finds anyone well.
- One ask per email. Two asks are two emails, or a numbered list the reader can answer by number.
- A greeting and a sign-off of one line each where the relationship expects them, and nothing else around the content.
- Transactional email carries no promotion.
- Result to record: the subject and the first line, and the count of asks.

## Notifications

- One event per notification. The text names the object and what happened to it: `{name} commented on {document}`.
- Actionable where possible: opening the notification lands on the object.
- No sensitive content in the part shown on a locked screen.
- No marketing in a notification about the person's own data or activity.
- Result to record: count of notifications without an object, and count carrying more than one event.

## Status reports and replies

For any report of work done and any answer to a question.

- The first line is what happened or what has to be done. No preamble, no closing, no offer to do more.
- Five lines at most, one fact per line. When more is needed, one table, then stop; the rest goes out when asked for (Practice).
- Only what changes the reader's next decision goes in: something broken, an assumption taken, something not verified, something the reader has to do. Work the reader asked for, done as asked, is not reported back (Practice).
- A yes-or-no question gets yes or no.
- A decision is offered as two options in one line with the one recommended.
- What was not verified is said: *builds, not tested in the browser* beats a false *done*.
- No restating of the request and no list of files touched.
- Result to record: line count, and each line tagged as broken, assumption, unverified, action needed or answer. An untagged line is cut.
