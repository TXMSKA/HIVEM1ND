# Protocol steps

What each step checks, in a few words. A run picks its steps here and opens a protocol only when one of its steps will run; a step with nothing to act on ends as not applicable from this line.

- [product-requirements](protocols/product-requirements.md): 1 sources listed; 2 problem and value sourced; 3 requirement lines and criteria; 4 out of scope sourced; 5 remaining sections and floors; 6 brief, boards and voice agree; 7 updated set and handed over.
- [requirement-acceptance](protocols/requirement-acceptance.md): 1 cited IDs exist; 2 evidence per criterion; 3 criterion observed again; 4 floors touched hold; 5 verdict and met count.
